ABOUT UNMILD

Hardware deserves hard tools.

UNMILD is a hardware-first product collection for people turning electrical ideas into real systems.

THE POINT OF VIEW

Software often treats hardware like an abstract diagram. UNMILD starts from the other end: a board is physical, firmware has consequences, and a good tool should make that reality easier to understand.

That is why UNMILD products are designed to keep the important parts of engineering close together. Design the physical system. Give it behavior. Observe what it does. Make the next decision with confidence.

Unmild Designer is the first product in the collection - a professional hardware-design environment that connects PCB work, Live 3D review, verification, and manufacturing release.
